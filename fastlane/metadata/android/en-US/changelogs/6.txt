Changed
• The overlay is a single row without labels: app logo (tap for settings, small gear), Share, Search, Translate, Close.
• Circling an area no longer opens a separate pill; Share, Search and Translate act on the circle and the framed Close drops it.
• The translate toolbar matches the main pill: same size, app logo with settings on the left, languages, framed Close.
• Search results: Share and a framed Close in the main pill's style.
• OCR and language settings are part of the Translator settings page.
• Search engines can be switched off one by one in Settings › Search.

Removed
• Translator (text input), Live cam, Gallery and Save buttons; the app no longer requests the camera permission.

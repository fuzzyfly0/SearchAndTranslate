Changed
• Image search without an image host: each results tab (Google, Bing, TinEye) uploads the image directly to its own search engine. No more ImageDB or Litterbox.
• New overlay layout in a 5-column grid: Translator, Live cam, Translate, Search and Gallery on top; settings, save, share and close below. Voice and Song search removed.
• Circle: Search and Translate act on the circled area (framed), Save and Share move into the main pill; the circle pill keeps only Close.
• Translate mode reuses the main pill: only the language switch and Close.
• Languages is its own main menu entry (source language and language packs).
• The launcher icon opens About while the accessibility permission is missing; the main menu shows a red mark on About until it is granted.
• Uses the system font instead of the bundled Google Sans Flex.

Fixed
• Back in the language picker closes the picker first; the picker has its own close button.
• Leaving the app during a translation ends it cleanly.
• The overlay no longer disappears after closing (bottom-edge triggers removed, they clashed with the home gesture).

Removed
• Bottom-edge triggers and the switch to turn Google search off.

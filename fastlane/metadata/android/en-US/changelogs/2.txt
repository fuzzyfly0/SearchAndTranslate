New
• Circle an area and pick Search or Translate right next to it. Translate replaces the text in place with the built-in offline translator.
• Setting "After circling an area" (Settings > Search): Ask, Always translate, Always search.
• Voice search: mic button in the search bar.
• Song search button: opens Shazam, SoundHound or the Google app.
• Share and Save buttons in the search results.
• New app icon.

Improved
• One Back closes the search results; Back first clears a selection or the Links & info view before closing the app.
• Clearer bottom bar labels: Live cam, Links & info, QR code, Search all, Live translate.
• Your cookie-banner choice on Google and Bing is remembered, so the consent dialog no longer appears on every search.
• The Share/Save/Pin toolbar no longer covers the controls.
• The Google Lens button shows a plain ring.

Fixed
• Live cam stayed black when no language pack was installed; it now opens the download screen.
• "Source code" was shown vertically on the app info screen.

Removed

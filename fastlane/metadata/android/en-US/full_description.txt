Search & Translate: draw a circle around anything on your screen, then search it or translate it right where it is.

SEARCH
• Circle to select, or search the whole screen
• After circling, choose Search or Translate (or set a default in Settings › Search)
• Image search with Google Lens, Bing and TinEye
• Voice search and song search (opens Shazam, SoundHound or the Google app)
• Share and save the circled area, also from the search results

TRANSLATE (OFFLINE)
• The circled area or the whole screen is translated and drawn over the original text
• Text recognition and translation run on your phone; language packs are downloaded once
• Live cam: translate text through the camera

MORE TOOLS
• Links & info: finds links, phone numbers and e-mail addresses on the screen (offline)
• QR code detection (offline)
• Tap words to select and copy text

START IT YOUR WAY
• Double-tap the status bar, floating bubble, quick-settings tile, hardware button or default assistant

PRIVACY
• Only the circled area is used. Translation, text recognition, QR codes and links & info stay on the device.
• Image search needs the internet: the circled area is uploaded to a temporary image host (deleted after 1 hour) so the search engine can load it. No tracking or analytics.
